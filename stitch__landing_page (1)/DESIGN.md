## Brand & Style
This design system embodies high-trust, mission-critical clarity tailored for enterprise obligation intelligence and automated legal/operational workflows. The visual identity merges hyper-refined Modern Corporate aesthetics with technical Precision Minimalism. It communicates absolute rigor, analytical depth, and proactive governance.

The interface evokes confidence, calm control, and institutional authority. Large micro-radii juxtaposed with spacious outer boundaries yield an architecture that feels modern, fluid, and structured. Visual noise is systematically eradicated to ensure complex contractual obligations, cross-organizational dependencies, and risk signals surface instantly without cognitive fatigue.

## Colors
The color foundation utilizes high-clarity slate tones over an luminous off-white canvas, accentuating automated intelligence and audit-grade decision pathways:

- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The base canvas floats between `#f8fafc` and `#fcfdff`. Containers utilize crisp, pure `#ffffff` to create clean dimensional layering without muddy separation.
- **Primary & Typography**: `#0f172a` (Deep Navy Slate) commands supreme hierarchical authority, ensuring maximum legibility across complex data sets. Secondary body text transitions through `#475569` and metadata to `#64748b`.
- **Accents & Machine States**: Precision electric blue (`#3b82f6`) provides interactive focus, while deep indigo (`#6366f1`) and soft violet (`#8b5cf6`) symbolize background AI synthesis, predictive tracking, and machine-derived insights.
- **Semantic Status Signals**:
  - *Active / Verified*: Emerald (`#10b981` fill / `#047857` text / `#ecfdf5` tint)
  - *Risk / Review Needed*: Amber (`#f59e0b` fill / `#b45309` text / `#fffbeb` tint)
  - *Blocked / Breach Alert*: Rose (`#f43f5e` fill / `#be123c` text / `#fff1f2` tint)
  - *Pending / Processing*: Sky to Indigo (`#0ea5e9` fill / `#0369a1` text / `#f0f9ff` tint)

## Typography
The system employs a dual-font structure: Geist for display, headings, metric cards, and functional UI metadata to emphasize structural precision; Inter for continuous narrative, legal clause summaries, and dense audit logs to ensure effortless legibility.

Headlines leverage tight negative tracking to maintain punchy editorial weight, while tabular data, numerical metrics, and micro-labels lean on Geist's crisp glyph shapes for absolute legibility at small dimensions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements a 12-column adaptive grid designed to accommodate multi-pane intelligence dashboards, clause inspectors, and interactive task pipelines:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px to 48px outer canvas margin. Workspace layouts prioritize a pinned sidebar or navigation spine, primary analytical canvas, and an optional collapsible AI intelligence inspector panel.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Panels collapse into layered modal sheets or swipeable tabs.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 12px gutters, 16px outer margin. Stacks all multi-column metrics and obligation matrices linearly.

Component vertical rhythm enforces strict internal consistency using the standard 4px/8px modular base scale to ensure tight tabular layouts alongside breathable data visualizations.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through crisp, high-luxe layered ambient shadows combined with hairline borders (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`), eschewing harsh outlines in favor of pure optical clarity:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#f8fafc` to `#fcfdff` surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: White `#ffffff` surface, border 1px solid `rgba(241, 245, 249, 1)`, shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State)**: White `#ffffff` surface, border 1px solid `rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.6)`, shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 10px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Clause Drawers, Command Palettes)**: Surface `#ffffff`, shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **AI Accent Glow**: Dynamic subtle drop shadows tinted with accent colors (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`) reserved exclusively for suggested automations, AI predictions, and high-impact risk mitigations.

## Shapes
The system balances large, architectural exterior shells with refined internal component radii:
- **Major Structural Containers**: Master dashboards, large workspace panels, and primary modal wrappers employ ultra-smooth corners (`24px` to `32px`).
- **Cards & Data Modules**: Standard operational cards, charts, and table wrappers utilize `16px` (spanning `14px`–`18px` contextually).
- **Controls & Form Elements**: Inputs, selection rows, and standard action buttons use `8px` to `10px` for clear affordance.
- **Badges & Status Micro-Tokens**: Complete pill geometry (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata and operational states from actionable controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Navy Slate (`#0f172a`) background, `#ffffff` text, subtle top inner-bevel highlight, `8px` border radius. Hover introduces subtle lift and transition to `#1e293b`.
- **AI Action**: Dynamic gradient fill from `#3b82f6` to `#6366f1` with `0 2px 8px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25)` shadow; reserved for automated analysis, obligation extraction, and clause generation.
- **Secondary**: `#ffffff` background with 1px hairline border in `#e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`. Hover state switches surface to `#f8fafc`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent fill, text `#475569`, active/hover fill `#f1f5f9`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Strict pill silhouette (`rounded-full`), `4px` vertical padding, `10px` horizontal padding.
- Consists of a colored micro-dot (`6px`), uppercase tracking (`label-sm`), and light tint background paired with high-contrast text:
  - *Active/Verified*: `#ecfdf5` background, `#047857` text, `#10b981` dot.
  - *Warning/Risk*: `#fffbeb` background, `#b45309` text, `#f59e0b` dot.
  - *Blocked/Breached*: `#fff1f2` background, `#be123c` text, `#f43f5e` dot.
  - *Pending*: `#f0f9ff` background, `#0369a1` text, `#0ea5e9` dot.

### Cards & Analytical Widgets
- Pure `#ffffff` surface resting on the `#f8fafc` canvas, rounded to `16px`.
- Card headers feature clean vertical alignment with typography token `title-md`, subtle gray action overflow triggers, and thin dividing rules (`#f1f5f9`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Solid `#ffffff` background, `1px` continuous border in `#e2e8f0`, text in `#0f172a` (`body-md`), placeholder text in `#94a3b8`. Focus state applies a crisp `0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)` ring and changes border to `#3b82f6`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: `6px` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Checked state fills with `#0f172a` (standard) or `#3b82f6` (automated selection).

### Lists & Data Tables
- Edge-to-edge row treatments with subtle bottom borders (`#f1f5f9`).
- Hover row state triggers a uniform background shift to `#f8fafc` with smooth ease transitions.
- Interactive obligation rows surface expandable drawers revealing AI-verified source citations, extraction confidence scores, and legal clause links.

### Specialized Component: AI Confidence & Obligation Pill
- A hybrid indicator composed of a dual-segment badge: the left side displays the automated state (e.g., "AI EXTRACTED"), and the right side displays a numerical confidence score (e.g., "99.4%") with an electric blue micro-bar underneath.
